🍛 Party Jollof Rice

Ingredients 🛒

  • 2 cups rice
  • 4 tomatoes
  • 2 onions
  • Pepper, salt and seasoning

Steps 👩‍🍳

  1. Blend the tomatoes, peppers and onions.
  2. Fry the mixture until the sauce thickens.
  3. Add washed rice and some water.
  4. Cover and cook on low heat.
  5. Serve and enjoy!

Tip: Don't open the pot too often.
